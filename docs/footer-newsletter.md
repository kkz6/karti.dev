# Footer newsletter

The shared public footer places the newsletter form at the bottom of its left-hand bio column, above the copyright divider. There is no separate newsletter banner, heading or introductory copy.

The email field and Subscribe button share a 44px height and remain inline. Below the desktop breakpoint, footer columns stack without changing the form controls. The field keeps a screen-reader label; validation and subscription feedback remain visible below the form when applicable. Honeypot, timing checks and double opt-in are unchanged.
